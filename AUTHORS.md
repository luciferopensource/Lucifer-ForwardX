## ⚔️ Core Subsystems & Attribution

- **MTProto Transport Layer:** Built upon Pyrofork (`pyrofork==2.3.45`) and Pyromod for advanced interactive Telegram MTProto bot and userbot workflows.
- **Asynchronous Database Engine:** Powered by MongoDB Motor async driver (`motor`) with connection pooling and state recovery.
- **Archive Extraction Engine:** Utilises 7-Zip (`7z`) for high-speed multi-part compressed archive extraction and unpacking.
- **Containerisation & Microservice Architecture:** Multi-stage Docker containers with C-extensions (`tgcrypto`, `uvloop`) for maximum throughput.
